# Retrieval Benchmark: Strategy A vs Strategy B

This report is generated by `scripts/run_benchmark.py`. It compares three retrieval strategies over the corpus in `data/corpus.py`:

- **Strategy A** — raw embedding similarity (baseline).
- **Strategy B (rewrite)** — a mocked `GenerativeModel` rewrites the query with domain synonyms before embedding.
- **Strategy B (HyDE)** — the mock produces three hypothetical answer passages; their embeddings are averaged and re-normalized, then searched.

Scores are cosine similarities in `[-1, 1]` (higher is better). `Overlap` is the number of top-3 doc IDs shared with Strategy A.
